Introduction to Computer Science and Applications

Study the supplied course units, take topic-based quizzes, and track your progress.

Unit 1 · Introduction to Information and Communication Technology (ICT)

Comprehensive notes, examination revision questions, and a question-bank quiz based on this unit.

Unit 2 · Software — Systems and Applications

Study software classifications, operating systems, utilities, device drivers, applications, and operating system comparisons.

Unit 3 · Personal and Mobile Technologies

Study the PDA era, tablets, personal-device comparisons, mobile technology, and its impact in Malawi.

Unit 4 · The Internet and World Wide Web

Study Internet history, the Web, common services, web-page loading, practical applications, and access challenges in Malawi.

Unit 5 · Information Systems

Study information-system components, organisational levels, TPS, MIS, DSS, GDSS, ESS, AIS, and their organisational value.

Unit 6 · Databases I — Concepts and Building in MS Access

Study database concepts, DBMS functions, keys and relationships, data integrity, and practical database construction in Access.

Unit 7 · Databases II — Queries and Reports in MS Access

Study query types, criteria, calculated fields, report creation, grouping, sorting, and the tables-to-reports workflow.

University of Malawi · Bachelor of Science in Statistics

ASC111: INTRODUCTION TO ACTUARIAL SCIENCE

Course units, lecture notes, and quizzes

Unit 1 · The Actuarial Profession

Comprehensive lecture notes, review questions, and four 20-question difficulty-level quizzes based on the supplied Unit 1 material.

Unit 2 · History of the Actuarial Profession

Study the origins of risk sharing, pioneers in probability and mortality, life insurance, actuarial institutions, and the modern profession.

Unit 3 · Basic Principles and Terminology

Review models, probability and statistics, risk pooling, the Law of Large Numbers, financial security systems, and actuarial risk management.

Unit 4 · Economics of Risk

Study economic loss, risk aversion, financial security systems, utility theory, and the role of actuaries.

Unit 5 · Random Variables

Learn probability distributions, moments, actuarial random variables, mortality studies, and credibility theory.

Unit 6 · Time Value of Money

Study interest, accumulation and discounting, simple and compound interest, and nominal and effective rates.
